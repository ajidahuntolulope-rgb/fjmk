The assessment

Ninety minutes
before we touch you.

01

Measure

Corneal topography, pachymetry, refraction, pressure, dilated fundus exam. We map the shape of your eye to the micron before anyone suggests surgery.

02

Tell you the truth

Roughly one in five people who come in for LASIK should not have it. Dry eye, a thin cornea, an unstable prescription — we will say so, and tell you what we would do instead.

03

Operate

Both eyes, same session, about fifteen minutes under the laser. You are awake. You will be walked out, not carried.

04

Follow up for years

Day one, week one, month one, then annually. Refractive surgery is a long relationship, not a transaction.
