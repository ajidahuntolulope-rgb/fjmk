A practical tool for clinics

A better first
look at your website.

A free live check for clinics in Lagos and across Nigeria. See the patient contact routes, search details and basic signals on the page they land on first.

Free homepage check · No sign-up

Start with the
front door.

Enter the public address for your clinic, hospital or pharmacy. We check the page people land on first.

The public address goes to FJMK’s scan service and, when available, Google PageSpeed Insights for a mobile Lighthouse lab snapshot. FJMK does not save a copy of the report. Cloudflare, Google and the website may process or record technical request data. Do not enter a patient portal, sign-in link or URL containing a private token.

What the first check covers

  • HTTPS and whether the homepage answers
  • Phone, WhatsApp and booking routes
  • Page title, description and indexing signals
  • Mobile viewport and image descriptions
  • Google PageSpeed mobile Lighthouse snapshot
  • Forms, outside scripts and browser security headers

One page. A useful first look.

This reads the homepage’s initial HTML and response headers, then requests an emulated mobile Lighthouse lab snapshot from Google PageSpeed Insights. Scores can vary and are not a measure of Lagos users’ experience. The check does not click through the site, test a form, or decide legal compliance. Lighthouse accessibility is not a WCAG audit.

A person should still check

Three questions the scanner cannot answer.

  • Can a patient on a phone find the right service, location and opening hours quickly?
  • Can they ask for an appointment without putting sensitive health details in a general form or chat?
  • Are the clinicians, services and claims on the page current and verifiable?

FJMK can review the full patient journey and turn the findings into a practical fix list.
