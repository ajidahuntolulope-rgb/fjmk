## The arbitrage, stated plainly

There is a specific gap in the Nigerian market:

**A large number of people have started businesses with capital** — from savings,
from family, from a previous job — and they are operating them without a
professional web presence. Meanwhile, building a good website has become
genuinely cheap to produce. That gap between what a website costs to make and
what a business will pay for one is the business.

**The healthcare angle is the wedge.** Clinics have money, have a clear
commercial reason to want patients, and mostly do not have a decent site. Once
we have healthcare references, the same play extends to every other sector with
cash and no website.

## Pricing context

Professional website builds in Nigeria run **₦150k–₦500k**; corporate builds
**₦500k–₦1.5M+**.

**The affordability argument:** for an IVF clinic doing ₦600k–₦5M per cycle, a
₦500k website is **less than one cycle's revenue** — and it is a one-time cost
against a recurring revenue stream. That is the sentence that closes the sale.

Our existing packages (₦150k Starter / ₦200k Growth / ₦300k+ Shop) sit at the
accessible end deliberately. The pricing is the entry point; the care plan is the
revenue.

verifiable. The same pitch — *your customers are searching and finding someone
else* — works in every sector above.

## Emerging niches worth watching

- **Mental health** — 40M+ Nigerians affected, under 10% access treatment. Very
  low digital competition. Growing acceptance.
- **Home nursing** — diaspora-driven demand. Families abroad paying for care at
  home. Almost nobody has a professional site.
- **Online pharmacy** — $1.97B market, 7% CAGR. Regulatory complexity is a moat
  once you understand it.
- **Medical tourism reversal** — outbound spending dropped 96% as local capacity
  grew, but the *domestic* medical tourism agency (matching patients to Nigerian
  hospitals) is barely served.
- **Diaspora healthcare** — Nigerians abroad arranging care for parents at home.
  A distinct, affluent, underserved audience that researches entirely online.
