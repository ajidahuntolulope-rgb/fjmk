AI vs Google for Clinics in Lagos: Google First, AI Second

A Google-first plan for Lagos clinics, using Nigeria search data, a local website screen and Google’s guidance on local and AI search.

FJMK · Healthcare design and digital practice · Published 25 October 2026

Short answer: If you are weighing AI vs Google for clinics in Lagos, do the Google work first. Claim and verify your Google Business Profile, set the right category, correct the address and opening hours, add a working phone, and link the profile to that branch’s page. Check it on a phone. Then put the same service and location details in readable text on your website. That order matters: AI visibility builds on clear, accessible information, not a separate trick.

Why Google comes first

In StatCounter’s September 2026 Nigeria panel, Google accounted for 98.28% of search-engine traffic. That is not a survey of patients or clinic choices. It does show which search engine dominates when people use web search.

Google’s local-ranking guidance says local results mainly reflect relevance, distance and prominence. It recommends verifying your profile and keeping your category, address and hours accurate. A complete profile helps Google match a clinic to relevant searches. For Lagos, get the exact branch and neighbourhood right before adding anything else.

What the lead data says

A 2026 Goodjuju Marketing report analysed 115,676 visits and 1,975 converted leads across 31 US property-management companies over 90 days. The lead data came from 25 accounts: Google Search, Map Pack and Direct/Maps accounted for 88.4% of leads; AI/LLM search produced 1.6%. AI visitors converted more often but brought far fewer leads. That is Google-first evidence for that sample, not Lagos healthcare. For a Lagos clinic, it points to a practical priority: complete the Google profile and the matching location page before investing in separate AI tactics.

What our Lagos check found

We checked the home pages of 20 Lagos provider websites. Twelve responded. Seven could not be retrieved and one returned a bot block, so they are not counted either way.

Of the twelve that responded, eleven had a page title, six had a meta description, ten had a phone-friendly viewport setting, and one carried structured data naming the business for search engines. Two had no canonical tag.

These are technical observations from public home pages on one day, not a ranking measurement, and they say nothing about the standard of care. Read narrowly, they show the machine-readable basics of a clinic site are often unfinished, which is the layer Google and AI systems read first.

The same groundwork helps AI

Google’s guidance for AI Overviews and AI Mode is clear: the same core SEO practices used for ordinary Google Search apply; there is no separate AI schema. That is a Google product statement, not a guarantee about ChatGPT or another chatbot. For clinic owners, it is a good reason to fix crawlable pages and accurate Business Profile details before paying for a separate “AI optimisation” package.

What to do this week

  1. Search the clinic’s exact name in Maps and claim or verify the existing profile; do not create a duplicate.
  2. Check each Lagos branch’s address pin, primary category, hours, phone and appointment route.
  3. Link the profile to that branch’s location or service page. On a phone, tap the website and call links to confirm they open the right place.
  4. Put the same service, location and contact details in readable text on the site, then review your profile and actual enquiries each month.

For profile setup, use the FJMK Google Maps guide. The free health website audit checks public homepage signals, including patient routes and search basics.

The practical decision is clear: put effort into the Google Business Profile and the pages behind it first. That work improves the clinic’s basic local-search presence and gives AI systems more accurate public information to work with.

Sources: StatCounter Nigeria search-engine traffic share; Google local-ranking guidance; Goodjuju 2026 lead-source report; Google guidance on AI features.
