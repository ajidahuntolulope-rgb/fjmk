# FJMK blog search research and editorial direction

Prepared 23 October 2026

## What the evidence can support

Search Console currently has no useful FJMK query baseline: the available hostname-filtered Performance view showed zero impressions and clicks. The Ahrefs keyword connector also returned `Insufficient plan`, so exact keyword volumes and difficulty are unavailable in this workspace. The searches below are therefore **intent and opportunity hypotheses**, not measured search volumes or confirmed rankings. Do not convert these phrases into volume claims.

Live result-page review shows commercial Nigerian pages already competing for broad phrases such as “hospital website design in Nigeria” and “healthcare website design Nigeria”. Their offers repeat a familiar feature list: mobile design, service pages, doctor profiles, booking forms and SEO. Searchers also ask practical questions such as how to add a clinic to Google Maps and why clinic websites get visits without enquiries. That makes broad service phrases more competitive and gives question-led articles a better near-term fit, while the main Services page owns the commercial offer.

Google’s guidance supports useful, original, accurate pages with clear structure and source attribution. Its AI Search guidance says existing SEO fundamentals apply and there is no special schema or technical shortcut for Google AI Overviews or AI Mode. The supplied LinkedIn/Meltwater study is a useful B2B publishing hypothesis about original expert voices and scannable structure; its citation figures and cadence recommendations are not evidence of Google ranking behaviour or healthcare demand.

## FJMK’s defensible editorial edge

**We connect discovery to the next workable step.** FJMK works across healthcare website design, content, search and practical systems. Our useful perspective is the handoff between a person finding a service, understanding whether it fits, trusting the information and contacting a team that can respond. Many agency pages stop at the website feature list. FJMK can explain and design the full route.

This is a point of view about how we approach the work, not proof of increased bookings. FJMK’s portfolio concepts are fictional, and the live checkup only reads limited HTML and headers. The articles must not turn either into client outcomes, conversion benchmarks or clinical authority.

## Editorial rules for these pages

1. State a direct answer immediately after the title.
2. Use question phrasing where it matches the searcher's task, followed by a concrete explanation rather than a keyword checklist.
3. Add an FJMK perspective only where it comes from the studio’s actual work or declared approach. Label concepts as concepts; do not invent client examples or results.
4. Link policy, platform and health-related claims to authoritative sources. Distinguish Google guidance from agency opinion and third-party research.
5. For health information, identify a real author and qualified clinical reviewer before claiming clinical review. These six articles are about digital practice and do not offer diagnosis or treatment.
6. Do not invent publication or review dates. Add them after a substantive editorial review is logged.
7. Keep the parked legacy articles outside this active six-page plan.

## Source review

- Google Search Central, [SEO Starter Guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/seo-starter-guide): readable, original, useful content; descriptive titles; anticipate readers’ language without targeting every query variation.
- Google Search Central, [Creating helpful, reliable, people-first content](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content): original information, insight beyond the obvious, accuracy, transparent authorship and purpose.
- Google Search Central, [AI features and your website](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/ai-features): standard SEO foundations apply; no extra technical requirements or special schema for Google’s AI Search features.
- Google Business Profile Help, [local ranking factors](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091), [profile representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177), and [review guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3474122).
- User-supplied LinkedIn/Meltwater article, “AI Search & LinkedIn: 5 Takeaways from 9.5 Million Citations” (21 May 2026). Useful for a cautious test of expert distribution and clear structure; not a ranking study for Google or health services.
- Search results reviewed 23 October 2026 included Nigerian healthcare-web-design service pages from [REWA Healthcare](https://rewa.com.ng/), [DPX Digital Network](https://dpx.com.ng/hospital-website-design-nigeria/) and [Linestech](https://www.linestech.com.ng/blog/hospital-website-design-in-nigeria/), plus clinic-specific Google Maps and conversion questions. This was a qualitative SERP scan, not a full competitor backlink or keyword-volume audit.
