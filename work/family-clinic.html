Our story

A clinic that feels like family.

Shomolu Family Clinic was founded on a simple idea: everyone deserves a doctor who knows them. Not your file number — you. We've been part of this community for over a decade, and we're proud to care for second-generation families.

  • Same-day appointments for urgent concerns
  • Doctors who take time to listen and explain
  • Affordable care with transparent pricing
  • Health records that follow you across visits
