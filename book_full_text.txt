 Creating Websites with AI 
Anyone can use AI to put a website online in minutes. But a site built in minutes is just any site – 
not one aligned to your goals, your visitors’ needs or the requirements of search engines and AI platforms. Te key is to use AI to get better results, not just faster results. 
Tis book provides comprehensive, practical guidance for beginners on leveraging AI to develop 
websites. Trough engaging conversations with AI tools like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude, read -
ers will learn how to create websites efciently and efectively. By remaining tool-agnostic, the content is adaptable to suit individual preferences and needs. 
Covering every essential aspect – from initial conception to marketing strategies – it ofers 
step-by-step instructions and numerous examples for immediate implementation. It intro-duces the principle of human-centred development, beginning with fundamental questions of website design, such as defning the goals of the website, understanding the target audience and aligning with their needs. Te book then guides readers in selecting the right tool for real -
ising the site, focusing on accessible platforms like Wix, Squarespace, WordPress and Webfow. With AI assistance, the book demonstrates how to create content, add it to the chosen platform and refne the results through feedback from humans and AI alike. It also explores how SEO and marketing strategies can be employed to optimise website visibility on search engines and AI platforms. 
Whether the goal is to design a site for a retail store, a medical practice, a restaurant, a personal 
portfolio or an organisation, this book equips readers with the tools and strategies to confdently develop websites tailored to their unique needs and objectives. 
Jens Jacobsen is a highly experienced author of practical, beginner-friendly guides and profes-
sional handbooks on web development, user experience (UX) and e-commerce. Across all his work, he is driven by one question: how to convey content in a way that truly resonates with read-ers. Beyond his books, he shares his knowledge in training courses and seminars and designs all kinds of websites, interactive applications for museums, apps and educational games.

1.4 WHY CREATE WEBSITES AT ALL? 
You may be wondering what role your own website will play in the future. Many people spend several hours a day on social media – whereas the amount of time spent visit -
ing websites has been declining for years. Large language models and AI assistants are already searching the web for specifc information for us and preparing it in a quick and easy-to-read format – all without us having to deal with dubious sites, click away annoy -
ing banners or separate content from advertising. More and more people are therefore no longer searching with Google or Bing, but with ChatGPT or Le Chat. In the medium term, such AIs could become our central point of contact for information, shopping and entertainment. We would then hardly ever see independent websites. AI shopping assis-tants search for products that are a perfect ft for us, fnd the shop with the best price and place the order without us ever having to visit an online shop ourselves. 
And yet your own website is and remains the only place online that you have complete 
control over. Social media platforms frequently change algorithms and terms of use; they come into fashion and become obsolete. AI providers operate closed systems, where you have no insight and cannot control them in any way. If you use third-party services, you have to play by their rules. And the providers who process your content first have to find it. Your website is indispensable for this. Just as we have had to make our content findable for search engines for decades, we now also have to make our content accessible to AI. 
When searching for information beyond short videos and text snippets, a central, 
reliable source is important. On your website, you present your services, products, references, values and standards – just as you  want to present them. Even if AI tools 
increasingly collect and process content, sources are evaluated, and high-quality, well-structured websites can score points here in the long term. Your own website is the means to digital sovereignty. It allows you to define your brand and convey your credibility. A

1.5 BUILDING WEBSITES FOR PEOPLE WITH AI 
Who decides whether your website is a success? Te people who use it. People need to visit your site, take in the information you provide (directly or via AI), contact you, book your services or buy your products. Tis means you need to engage with the people for whom you are developing the site. Tat’s why today’s really good websites (and apps and products in general) are created using human-centred development . To do this, you 
need to take a detailed look at everyone you want to reach with your site – your target groups. And AI is a great help here – the large language models have been trained with vast amounts of data about people, have seen millions of websites and can thus access an immeasurable treasure trove of information about what people want and do on the web. 
This is another reason why AI is so helpful in planning the user experience  (UX). 
User experience encompasses all aspects of a person’s interaction with a product – from their expectations to their feelings during use and to their experiences afterwards. Usability , i.e. user- friendliness , is an important part of this and describes how easily
