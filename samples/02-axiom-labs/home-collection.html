Home collection

We come to you.
In a two-hour
window.

Twelve phlebotomists on the road across Lagos, six days a week. You get a named collector, a photo before they arrive, and a two-hour window rather than a whole-day promise.

01

You book a slot

Pick a two-hour window that suits you. Morning slots fill fastest, so book a day ahead if you can.

02

You meet your collector

You receive the name and photograph of the phlebotomist assigned to you, along with an arrival estimate.

03

Results, same as the lab

Samples go straight to the same analysers. Turnaround is identical to visiting a centre.

What a home visit costs

A flat ₦7,500 per visit anywhere in our Lagos coverage area, added to the price of your tests. No distance surcharge within the zone, no per-test fee, no separate handling charge.

Where we go

We currently cover the mainland and island zones below. If you are just outside, contact us — we often can, and we will tell you honestly if the sample will be compromised by travel time.

What to expect

  • Your collector carries a cold chain box and single-use, sealed consumables opened in front of you.
  • Most collections take ten minutes. Fasting tests need you to have fasted for eight hours — water is fine.
  • If a sample is compromised in transit we re-collect at no cost. It happens rarely and it is our problem, not yours.
  • You will receive a receipt and a tracking reference by SMS before the collector leaves.

Who this is for

People with a chronic condition who need regular monitoring and cannot keep taking half a day off work. Parents of small children, where a trip to a lab is its own ordeal. Elderly patients for whom a journey across Lagos is genuinely difficult. Anyone who would rather not sit in a waiting room.
