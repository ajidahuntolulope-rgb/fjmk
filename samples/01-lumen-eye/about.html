The institute

We only do
eyes. That is
the whole point.

Lumen opened in 2019 with two consulting rooms, one laser, and a conviction that a dedicated eye institute is a different thing from an eye department.

Why dedicated

Volume makes
you careful.

A general hospital treats eyes alongside a hundred other things. That is appropriate for a general hospital. But refractive surgery has a learning curve that never flattens, and surgeons who perform it weekly rather than yearly get better outcomes.

Our four consultants do nothing else. The equipment is calibrated for eyes. The nursing team only preps eyes. The follow-up protocol is built for eyes.

It also means we can afford to say no. About one in five people who come to us for LASIK should not have it. A general practice has every incentive to find something else to sell you. We would rather send you away with a clear explanation.
