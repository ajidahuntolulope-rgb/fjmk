Policy

Prescription Policy

What Needs a Prescription

  • Antibiotics — Amoxicillin, Azithromycin, Ciprofloxacin
  • Chronic Medication — Metformin, Amlodipine, Atorvastatin, Insulin
  • Respiratory — Salbutamol inhalers, Steroid inhalers
  • Thyroid — Levothyroxine, Carbimazole
  • Antiepileptics — Carbamazepine, Sodium Valproate

What Doesn't Need a Prescription

  • Pain Relief — Paracetamol, Ibuprofen (OTC doses)
  • Allergy Relief — Cetirizine, Loratadine
  • Vitamins & Supplements — Vitamin C, D3, Omega-3, Biotin
  • Personal Care — Sunscreen, hand gel, creams
  • First Aid — ORS sachets, bandages, antiseptics
