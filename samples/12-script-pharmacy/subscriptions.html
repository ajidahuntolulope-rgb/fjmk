Refills

How Refills Work

Automatic Dispensing

Based on your prescription and schedule, we automatically dispense your next pack before you run out. No reminders needed from you.

You'll receive a notification 3 days before each dispatch, with tracking details once your order is on its way.

Prescription Renewals

When your prescription expires, we contact your doctor directly for a renewal. You'll be notified if a new consultation is required.

We maintain a direct line to your healthcare provider to ensure zero gaps in your medication supply.
