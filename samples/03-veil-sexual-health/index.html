How Confidentiality Works

Your privacy is the treatment

We built Veil around one principle: you deserve care without exposure. Every process — from booking to results — is designed so your visit remains entirely your own.

No Names on Results

Your results are coded, not named. Only you and your clinician know the link. We never send results by post, phone, or shared email.

Discrete Entrance

Our Victoria Island entrance is unmarked. Separate waiting areas. No signage that says "sexual health clinic." You walk in like you belong somewhere else — because you do.

Appointment-Only

No walk-ins, no queues, no chance encounters. You book a slot, you're seen promptly, and you leave without crossing paths with anyone you know.

Data Protection

Records are encrypted, access is logged, and nothing is shared with employers, insurers, or family members without your written, informed consent.

The moment you realise no one is watching, no one is judging — that's the moment healing starts.
— Dr. A. Okafor, Medical Director

The Testing Process

Four steps. Zero exposure.

1

Book Online or by Phone

Choose a time that suits you. We offer early morning and evening slots for maximum discretion. No reason needed at booking.

2

Arrive & Check In

Use the side entrance on Adetokunbo Ademola Street. Give your first name or booking code — nothing more. You'll be seen within 5 minutes.

3

Consultation & Sample Collection

A clinician discusses your concerns privately, then collects samples. Most tests need only a urine sample or a quick blood draw. Painless, professional, over in minutes.

4

Results & Next Steps

Results arrive in 24–72 hours via a secure portal or encrypted message. If treatment is needed, we prescribe on the spot — or refer you to a trusted specialist, always with your consent.
