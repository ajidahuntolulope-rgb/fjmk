Why four and not forty.

We could list thirty destinations. We list four, because these are the four where we have visited the hospitals, met the consultants, and sent enough patients to know how it actually goes.

India for cardiac and orthopaedic surgery at the lowest cost. Turkey for IVF and hair restoration. The UAE for anything where you want to be four hours from home. The UK for complex oncology and for patients with existing NHS connections.

We are an agency, not a medical provider. Treatment is delivered by the hospital you choose. We are paid by you, not by the hospitals, so our recommendation is not for sale.

Fig. 03 — The process

Six steps,
start to finish.

01

Free consultation

We listen to what is wrong, look at whatever records you have, and tell you honestly whether travelling is worth it for your case. Sometimes the answer is no, and we say so.

02

Records review

Your scans, reports and history are sent to two or three shortlisted hospitals for a consultant opinion and a treatment quote. This usually takes five to ten days.

03

Hospital match

We present the options with the quotes side by side, and our recommendation. You choose. We do not take a commission from the hospital — our fee is paid by you, which is why we can say no.

04

Visa & travel

Invitation letter, visa application, flights, airport transfer and accommodation. We handle the paperwork and tell you exactly what to bring to the embassy.

05

Treatment

A named coordinator meets you at the destination airport and takes you to the hospital. They stay reachable for the whole trip, including for your companion.

06

Aftercare

Discharge summary, medication plan and a follow-up appointment arranged with a Lagos consultant who can take over your care when you are home.
